Business Sales Performance Analytics

Project Overview:
This project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data to understand business performance, identify trends, and generate actionable insights. The goal is to help improve revenue, profitability, and regional performance using data-driven decisions.

Tools Used:
Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning)
Power BI (Data Visualization & Dashboarding)
DAX (KPI Calculations)

Dataset:
Superstore sales data from kaggle

Business Questions:
Which products generate the most revenue?
How do sales change over time?
Which categories or regions are most profitable?
Where should the business focus to grow faster?

Key Insights:
Top Products: The 3D Systems Cube Printer ($9.1K) is the highest revenue generator, with the top 5 products contributing ~13% of total sales (~$23K).
Sales Trends: Revenue peaks in April ($27K) but drops sharply in July–August (~$7K), showing strong seasonality.
Categories: Technology (37%) leads sales, followed by Office Supplies (33%) and Furniture (30%).
Regions: West (32%) and East (28%) dominate, while South (20%) and Central (21%) underperform.

Business Recommendations:
Improve profitability by focusing on high-margin products and optimized pricing.
Reduce seasonal impact through better planning and targeted marketing campaigns.
Increase revenue using product bundling and promotion of top-selling items.
Expand business in South and Central regions to balance growth.
Strengthen focus on Technology products while improving weaker categories.

Business Impact:
By implementing these strategies, the business can achieve:
More stable revenue across months.
Improved profit margins (11% → 15%).
Better regional sales balance.
Reduced dependency on top products.
